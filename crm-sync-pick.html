Choose your scorecard

CRM Sync can only open the Google Sheet you choose here. Nothing else in your Google Drive is shared with it.

  1. Click Choose sheet in Google and sign in with the same Google account you connected in CRM Sync.
  2. Google shows your scorecard. Click it, then Select.
  3. This tab closes and CRM Sync carries on.

This page uses Google's own sign-in and file picker. It doesn't store anything; it only tells CRM Sync which sheet you chose. Privacy policy
